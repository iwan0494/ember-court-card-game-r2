THE COURT IS IN SESSION

EMBER COURT

a duel of suits and cunning

You and Lady Cinder start at 30 life. Spend 3 energy a turn. Play two cards of the same suit in one turn for a COMBO ×2. Court cards hit twice as hard. Reduce Cinder to 0 to claim the court.

Keys: 1–5 select · Enter play · Esc cancel · E end turn · R restart

How to Play

  • 1–5 — select a card in your hand. Enter plays it, Esc cancels.
  • Each card costs energy. You get 3 energy every turn.
  • Playing two cards of the same suit in one turn triggers COMBO ×2 damage on the second card.
  • Court cards (Queen / King) deal double damage.
  • Press E to end your turn — Cinder answers with her own hand.
  • First to 0 life loses. Cinder at 0 means the court is yours.
